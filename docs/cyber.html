THE CHRONICLES

2019

The Original Hunt

Retired blade runner Rick Deckard is forced to resume his job. Four replicants have escaped from off-world colonies and returned to Earth, seeking their creator to extend their four-year lifespan.

2022

The Blackout

A massive electromagnetic pulse wipes out most of Earth's electronic records. The true origins of many replicants, and potentially their creators, become lost to history.

2036

Wallace's Rise

Niander Wallace Sr. acquires the bankrupt Tyrell Corporation. His breakthroughs in genetic engineering allow for the production of new, more obedient replicants.

2049

The Discovery

Blade runner K uncovers a long-buried secret that has the potential to plunge what's left of society into chaos. His discovery leads him to track down Deckard, who's been missing for thirty years.

WHO IS REAL

RICK DECKARD
Blade Runner / Possible Replicant

A retired blade runner who specialized in "retiring" replicants. His encounter with Rachael and the Nexus-6 models challenges everything he believes about humanity and artificial life.

K
Blade Runner / Nexus-9

A dedicated blade runner for the LAPD. Unlike earlier models, K was designed to be obedient. His discovery of a mystery leads him on a journey to find Deckard and question his own identity.

Blade Runner 2049

CORE THEMES

🧠

IDENTITY & HUMANITY

What makes us human? Is it our memories, our emotions, our physical bodies, or something deeper? The replicants possess memories, feel fear and love, and desire more life - challenging our definitions of humanity itself.

"All those moments will be lost in time, like tears in rain."

💭

MEMORY & TRUTH

Memories define who we are, but what happens when they're implanted? The films explore how our experiences - real or artificial - shape our identity. Can false memories still create real meaning?

"We're all just looking for something real."

🌂

MORTALITY & LEGACY

The replicants' four-year lifespan forces confrontation with mortality. What gives life meaning when it's finite? What traces do we leave behind, and do our stories matter in the endless flow of time?

"I've seen things you people wouldn't believe..."

🎨

VISION & REALITY

From the Voight-Kampff test to the baseline, the films question how we perceive reality. Eyes are everywhere - a metaphor for seeing but not understanding, watching but not truly observing.

"It's too bad she won't live. But then again, who does?"
